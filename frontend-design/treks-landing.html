Insight Nepal

A Country Built From Mountains

Nepal packs eight of the world's fourteen 8,000m peaks into a country smaller than England, rising from tropical lowlands to the roof of the world in under 200 kilometres.

That compression is what makes trekking here unlike anywhere else — a single trail can pass through subtropical forest, terraced farmland, pine and rhododendron, and high alpine desert in the space of a week.

It's also a living culture: Sherpa, Gurung, Thakali and Tamang communities have shaped these valleys for centuries, and the teahouses, monasteries and mani walls you pass are still very much in use, not museum pieces.
